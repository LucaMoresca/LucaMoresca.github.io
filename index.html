01 · about

AI and visual systems,
from research to teaching.

I'm a Computer Science graduate student and research collaborator interested in the behaviour of intelligent systems. My current work at PINlab, Sapienza University of Rome, examines large language models, bias analysis, efficient generation methods, and evaluation protocols.

Before moving into AI research, I trained in Media Science and Technology and developed a practical foundation in digital image processing, post-production, and computer vision. I now teach the third module of Digital Cinematography + Digital Image Processing at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, bringing together theory, perception, colour, and hands-on practice.

I enjoy problems where rigorous modelling meets a clear visual or human-facing outcome — whether that means probing language-model behaviour, building an experiment pipeline, or making images work harder.
