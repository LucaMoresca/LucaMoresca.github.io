title: Research
summary: Research interests in language-model evaluation, reliability, and machine learning systems.
---

## LLM Evaluation

I am interested in evaluating language models across behaviours, capabilities,
and failure modes, with attention to the design of evaluation protocols.

## AI Safety and Reliability

I study questions around reliable model behaviour, safety-relevant evaluation,
and the limits of claims drawn from benchmark results.

## Model Steering and Interpretability

I explore methods for analysing and steering model behaviour, including the
interpretation of internal representations and intervention-based experiments.

## Evaluation Awareness

I am interested in whether awareness of an evaluation context changes model
behaviour, and how that possibility should affect the interpretation of results.

## Benchmarking and Reproducibility

I work on methods for evaluating systems with documented assumptions, clear
protocols, reproducible environments, and appropriately stated limitations.

## Efficient Inference and ML Systems

I am interested in efficient GPU inference, remote and HPC environments,
containerized workloads, and the engineering required to run evaluations
reliably.
